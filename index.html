A sanctuary above the mist

Baya Weaver Resorts is being built as an eco-luxury retreat in Old Vythiri, where indigenous architecture, responsible hospitality and the Western Ghats meet in stillness.

Fourteen private villas sit on 3.27 acres of Highland Tea Estate, framed by birdsong and highland sky. Four villas have their own plunge pools, and one open pool serves the wider resort.

Every detail considered

From land title to ecological ethics, Baya Weaver is built on principles that protect owners, guests and the environment alike.

Clear hereditary title

Janmam clear-title land with zero encumbrances. Documentation is available to verified investors.

4 private pools + 1 open pool

Four villas each have their own plunge pool, not shared and not communal. One open pool serves the wider resort.

Eco-responsible design

Sustainable construction, native landscaping, solar integration and rainwater harvesting.

Grand clubhouse

A central clubhouse designed for dining, gatherings and recreation: the social heart of the resort.

National highway access

Near the NH corridor in Old Vythiri, with road connections to Calicut, Mysore and Bangalore.

Strong ROI potential

Wayanad tourism is among Keralam's fastest-growing segments, with limited comparable eco-luxury supply in Vythiri.

Old Vythiri, Wayanad

Address
Old Vythiri, Wayanad District, Keralam 673576. 3.27 acres of Highland Tea Estate on the National Highway corridor.
By road
About 70 km from Calicut (Kozhikode), 90 km from Mysore and 270 km from Bangalore via NH766.
Nearest airport
Calicut International Airport (CCJ), about 80 km or 90 minutes by road.
Elevation and climate
About 800 m above sea level. Cool mornings, a highland monsoon and crisp winter skies.
Get directions

11.5350° N, 76.0387° E

Nearby

  • Chembra PeakWayanad's highest summit. The trek to its heart-shaped lake is iconic.
  • Banasura Sagar DamIndia's largest earthen dam, ringed by misty hills.
  • Edakkal CavesStone-age petroglyphs high on Ambukuthi Mala.
  • Soochipara FallsA 200 ft cascade through rainforest, at its best after the monsoon.
  • Ennooru Tribal Heritage VillageA place to learn about Adivasi culture and traditions.
