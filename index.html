Old Vythiri, Wayanad

Baya Weaver Resorts is in Old Vythiri (also spelled Vaithiri), in the Wayanad district of Kerala, on the National Highway corridor between Kozhikode and Kalpetta. The 3.27-acre site sits on Highland Tea Estate at about 800 m above sea level, about 67 km (around 1 hour 20 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes by road) from Calicut International Airport.

Address
Old Vythiri, Wayanad District, Kerala 673576. 3.27 acres of Highland Tea Estate on the National Highway corridor.
By road
About 60 km from Kozhikode (Calicut), 150 km from Mysore and 295 km from Bangalore via NH766.
Nearest airport
Calicut International Airport (CCJ), about 67 km or 1 hour 20 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes by road.
Elevation and climate
About 800 m above sea level. Cool mornings, a highland monsoon and crisp winter skies.

  • Lakkidi View PointThe viewpoint at the top of the ghat road that climbs into Wayanad from Kozhikode.
  • Chembra PeakWayanad's highest summit. The trek to its heart-shaped lake is iconic.
  • Banasura Sagar DamIndia's largest earthen dam, ringed by misty hills.
  • Edakkal CavesStone-age petroglyphs high on Ambukuthi Mala.
  • Soochipara FallsA 200 ft cascade through rainforest, at its best after the monsoon.

What is Baya Weaver Resorts?

Baya Weaver Resorts is a new resort under construction in Old Vythiri, Wayanad, Kerala, India. It will have 14 villas on 3.27 acres of Highland Tea Estate, four of them with private plunge pools, plus an open pool and a clubhouse. The resort is designed by Sumayyasalih Architects and has a target opening of Q4 2026.

Who is the resort for?

Baya Weaver is planned for families, couples and nature lovers looking for a quiet highland stay in Wayanad, among tea gardens and forest in the Western Ghats.

How many villas are there, and which have private pools?

There are 14 villas on 3.27 acres. Four villas have their own private plunge pools, and one open pool serves the wider resort. The designs shown include the 2BHK Pool Villa and the Single Bed Villa.

What facilities will the resort have?

Alongside the 14 villas, the resort plans an open pool and a central clubhouse for dining, gatherings and recreation. Four villas have private plunge pools. The design includes native landscaping, solar integration and rainwater harvesting.

Where is the resort and how do I get there?

Old Vythiri, Wayanad District, Kerala 673576, on the National Highway corridor. Calicut International Airport is about 67 km away (around 1 hour 20 minutes to 1 hour 30 minutes by road). By road it is about 60 km from Kozhikode, 150 km from Mysore and 295 km from Bangalore via NH766.

Is Vythiri the same as Vaithiri?

Yes. Vythiri is also spelled Vaithiri. Old Vythiri is part of the Vythiri area in Wayanad district, Kerala, on the road between Kozhikode and Kalpetta.
