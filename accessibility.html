This statement covers the Baya Weaver Resorts website.

Our commitment

We want everyone to be able to learn about Baya Weaver Resorts and get in touch with us, whatever device or assistive technology they use. We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Text and controls are designed to meet WCAG AA contrast in both the light and dark themes. The site follows your system theme and you can switch it.
  • Images have text descriptions. Architectural renders and site photos are labelled so they are not confused.
  • Animations are switched off if you have asked your device to reduce motion, and nothing plays automatically.
  • Form fields have visible labels, and errors are described in text and announced to screen readers.
  • The mobile menu and the image viewer manage keyboard focus and close with the Escape key.
  • Pages keep working if JavaScript is off, and the layout adapts to small screens and to zoom.
  • If you ask your system for more contrast, secondary text is shown at full strength.

Known limitations

  • The project video and the map are provided by YouTube and Google Maps. Their players are outside our control and may not meet the same standard.
  • The project video does not yet have captions or a transcript.
  • Some architectural renders contain small watermark text from the architects that is not described in full.

We are working to address these.

How we test

We check the website with keyboard-only navigation, with reduced motion and high contrast settings, with JavaScript turned off, and at screen widths from 360 pixels upwards, in both themes. We have not yet completed testing with every screen reader, and we welcome reports from people who use one.

Tell us about a problem

If something on this website is hard to use, or you need information in a different format, please email bayaweaverresort@gmail.com with “Accessibility” in the subject line. Tell us the page and what went wrong, and we will do our best to help and to fix it.
