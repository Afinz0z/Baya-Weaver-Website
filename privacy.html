This policy explains what personal data we collect through this website, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We collect only what you choose to send us through the forms or by email.
  • We use it to reply to you and, if you ask for them, to send construction updates.
  • We do not sell your details, and this website has no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.
  • YouTube and Google Maps load only when you press play or ask for the map.
  • You can ask us to see, correct or delete your details at any time: bayaweaverresort@gmail.com.

Who we are

This website is run by Baya Weaver Resorts, a resort under development in Old Vythiri, Wayanad District, Kerala 673576, India (“we”, “us”). We decide how the personal data collected through this website is used. You can reach us at bayaweaverresort@gmail.com.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Enquiry form: your first and last name, email address, phone number (optional), the type of enquiry, a preferred visit date (optional) and your message.
  • Register interest: your email address.
  • Email: anything you include when you write to us directly.

Information collected automatically

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record technical information such as your IP address when you visit, for security and to keep the service running. We do not receive these records, and we do not run analytics or advertising tools on this website.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiry and answer your questions.
  • To arrange a site visit if you ask for one.
  • To send the brochure or information you request.
  • To send construction and launch updates if you registered for them.
  • To keep records of our conversations with you, and to meet legal obligations.

We do not use your details for automated decision-making, and we do not sell or rent them to anyone.

Your consent

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, which you give by sending us an enquiry or registering your email for updates. This is in line with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing bayaweaverresort@gmail.com, and we will stop using your details for that purpose. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.

Who we share it with

We share your details only with the services we need to run this website and our inbox:

  • Formspree, which receives form submissions and forwards them to us by email.
  • Google, which provides our email inbox (Gmail).
  • GitHub, which hosts this website.

These providers handle data under their own privacy terms. We may also share information where the law requires it, for example in response to a lawful request from a public authority.

Where your data is processed

Formspree, Google and GitHub may store and process data on servers outside India, including in the United States. By using the forms, you understand that your details may be transferred there for the purposes described in this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to deal with your enquiry and any follow-up, and then delete them. If you registered for updates, we keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it. If the law requires us to keep certain records for longer, we keep them only for that purpose.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it;
  • stop sending you updates;
  • deal with a complaint about how we handled your data;
  • record someone you nominate to act for you in case of death or incapacity.

Email bayaweaverresort@gmail.com to make a request. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies and storage on your device

This website does not set cookies. It stores two small things in your own browser, which never leave your device:

  • Theme choice (light or dark), saved in local storage so the site remembers it.
  • An unsent enquiry, saved in session storage so a draft survives an accidental reload. It is deleted when you close the tab or once your enquiry is sent.

You can clear both at any time in your browser settings.

Videos, maps and other websites

The project video and the map are provided by YouTube and Google Maps. Nothing is loaded from them until you press play or choose “Show map”. When you do, Google may collect information under its own privacy policy. The video uses YouTube’s privacy-enhanced mode.

Links to other websites, such as YouTube, Google Maps and nearby attractions, are provided for convenience. We are not responsible for how those websites handle your data.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us their details, please contact us and we will delete them.

Keeping your data safe

This website is served only over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we limit access to enquiries to the people who need them to respond. No method of sending or storing data online is completely secure, so please do not send sensitive information such as identity documents or bank details through the forms.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when our practices change, for example if we add new services. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed.

Contact and grievances

For any question, request or complaint about your personal data, email bayaweaverresort@gmail.com. Please include “Privacy” in the subject line so it reaches the right person quickly.
